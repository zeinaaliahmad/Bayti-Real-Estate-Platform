What happens after submission?

Reports are reviewed based on listing details, local context, comparable properties, and repeated complaints.

Useful evidence to include

  • Links to similar nearby properties with lower prices
  • Older screenshots showing previous prices
  • Notes on poor condition or misleading listing details
  • Evidence of crisis-related price inflation
