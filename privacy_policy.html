Privacy Policy

How Bayti handles your data, protects your information, and supports a safer digital real estate experience in Lebanon.

Last updated: April 2026

At Bayti, privacy is part of trust. This page explains what data we collect, why we collect it, how it is used, and the choices available to you when using our platform.

What we collect

  • Basic account details such as name, email, phone number, and login credentials.
  • Property listing details, saved homes, messages, and preferences.
  • Platform usage data that helps improve search, security, and performance.

Why we collect it

  • To create and manage your account.
  • To help buyers, renters, and sellers interact safely.
  • To reduce fraud, detect suspicious activity, and improve transparency.

How we protect it

  • Secure authentication and controlled access to account features.
  • Encrypted data transmission where applicable.
  • Internal monitoring to detect misuse, abuse, or unauthorized access.

Your choices

  • Update your personal information in your account settings.
  • Request help regarding stored information through our contact channels.
  • Control certain cookies and non-essential tracking preferences.

Information sharing

Bayti does not sell your personal data. Information may be shared only when needed to operate platform services, respond to legal obligations, support security, or deliver a requested feature such as agent contact or listing communication.

Data retention

We keep information only for as long as necessary to provide services, maintain platform integrity, comply with legal obligations, and resolve disputes where needed.
