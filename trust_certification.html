Trust Certification

A Bayti standard designed to encourage transparency, listing quality, and safer real estate decisions in Lebanon.

Bayti Trust Framework

Trust Certification highlights listings and agents that meet stronger standards for clarity, reliability, and user confidence. It helps users quickly identify homes that provide more transparent information.

What it means

A trust-certified listing may include more complete property information, consistent image quality, verified contact details, and stronger pricing clarity.

What we look for

  • Complete and accurate property details
  • Transparent pricing information
  • Clear contact identity
  • Reduced signs of misleading or abusive listing behavior

Why it matters

In a complex housing market, trust signals reduce uncertainty and help people browse with more confidence, especially during urgent home searches.

Important note

Trust Certification is a platform-level confidence signal, not a legal property guarantee. Users should still verify all documents and agreements before making decisions.

Bayti vision

This feature reflects Bayti’s commitment to fairness and transparency in Lebanese real estate. It is part of a broader effort to reduce misleading information and improve trust between owners, renters, buyers, and agents.
