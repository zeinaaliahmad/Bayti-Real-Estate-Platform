AI Insights Guide

Understand how Bayti uses intelligent tools to support pricing clarity, better browsing, and more informed housing decisions.

Transparent by design

Bayti AI features are designed to assist users, not replace judgment. They help surface useful insights such as pricing context, listing comparisons, and search support.

Possible AI features

  • Price explanation and market context
  • Personalized home recommendations
  • Natural-language search assistance
  • Detection of unusual listing patterns

What AI should never do alone

  • Guarantee a property’s legal or physical condition
  • Replace document verification
  • Make financial decisions on behalf of users

How users should read AI insights

Treat Bayti AI as a support layer. It gives context, comparisons, and recommendations, but final decisions should always consider documents, inspections, agent discussions, and real market conditions.

Why this matters

In Lebanon’s housing market, transparent explanations can reduce confusion and help people spot unusually priced or incomplete listings more easily.

Example

User prompt: “Why is this apartment priced higher than similar homes nearby?”

Bayti AI insight: “This listing may be priced higher due to larger area, newer finishes, backup electricity features, and location advantages. Compare it with nearby homes before deciding.”
